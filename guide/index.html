Getting started

What is Diamond DJ?

A soundboard for youth baseball and softball volunteers. It plays each batter's announcement and walk-up song, plus colour-coded music for the rest of the game: warm-up, pitchers, crowd favourites and post-game celebrations.

It's a web page that installs like an app. Everything plays from the device, so no wifi is needed at the field. Internet is only needed to install it, download the team and get updates.

Made for an Android tablet. Phones and iPads work too.

How do I install it on an Android tablet or phone?

  1. Open scalisec.github.io/diamond-dj in Chrome.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings), tap Install Diamond DJ at the top, then Install.
  3. If the button shows steps instead: tap Chrome's menu ⋮ (top right) › Add to Home screen (or Install app) › Install.
  4. Close Chrome and open Diamond DJ from the Bees icon from now on.

Samsung Internet: menu ≡ › Add page to › Home screen. Chrome works best.

How do I install it on an iPad or iPhone?

  1. Open scalisec.github.io/diamond-dj in Safari (other browsers on iPad can't install apps).
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow: at the top on iPad, at the bottom on iPhone).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Always open it from the Home Screen icon. Safari can clear a website's saved songs if it isn't on the Home Screen.

No sound with the ring/silent switch on silent? Turn silent off, or update iOS.

It says “already installed” but won't open.

This can happen on an Android phone when Diamond DJ is installed on another device signed in to the same Google account: Chrome thinks this phone has it too.

  1. In Chrome, open the web address, tap ⋮ › Add to Home screen.
  2. Choose Create shortcut (not Install), then Add.

The shortcut opens Diamond DJ and works the same way, offline too.

How do I get my team's roster and songs?

Your organizer shares the team's Google Drive folder with your Google account. Then, on wifi:

  1. Settings › Google Drive › Download from Google Drive (later it says Check for updates).
  2. Sign in with the Google account the folder was shared with. Google brings you back to the app.
  3. Tick the team (or teams) you run on this device and tap Download now.
  4. Keep the app open until it says Up to date.

Only new or changed songs are downloaded next time, so updates are quick.

What are the screens for?

The Game screen is where the music happens: Up next, Play walkup, the batting order and the Music. Pick the lineup above the batting order; the pencil next to Batting order edits the order. Every batter is listed (on a phone, Show just the next 3 shortens it).

Everything else is behind the gear, in Settings:

Manage
LineupsSaved batting orders: new, rename, reorder, bench, use for the game.
Team setupTeam organizers: roster and walk-ups, team songs, Music, team info, Publish.
AdminDiamond DJ Admin: the common songs and music categories every team uses.
Settings
UpdatesCheck for updates from Google Drive.
SoundFade length, walk-up length, the announcement mix.
This deviceInstall, storage, version, Help and what’s new.
AdvancedView as, backup files.

The ? at the top opens Help. A volunteer only sees Lineups under Manage.

Game day

What should I do before the game?

  1. At home on wifi: Settings › Check for updates.
  2. Charge the tablet. Turn on Do not disturb so notifications don't play through the speaker.
  3. At the field: connect the speaker (Bluetooth or cable) first, then open Diamond DJ from its icon.
  4. On Game, check the lineup at the top is today's. Tap New game twice.
  5. Tap Lock.

How do I play a walk-up?

The big card at the top of Game shows who is Up next. When they walk to the plate, tap Play walkup: their announcement plays, then their song. The song stops by itself at its stop point (or after the walk-up length in Settings) with a fade.

The order moves on to the next batter by itself and goes back to the top after the last one. Skip passes over the batter up next without playing anything.

A batter comes up out of order.

  • Tap any batter in the list to play their walk-up now. The order carries on from them.
  • Press and hold a batter to make them up next without playing anything.

How do I change the order during a game?

On Game, tap the pencil next to Batting order (Edit order). Drag a batter by the handle (or use the arrows) to move them, Bench to take them out, and Add back to bring them back. It saves as you go; tap Done.

This changes the lineup the game is using. The same lineup is in Settings › Lineups.

What do New game and Start from batter 1 do?

New game (tap it twice) goes back to batter 1 and clears the Played marks in the Music. Use it at the start of each game.

Start from batter 1 only moves Up next back to the top of the order.

How does the Music work?

Next to the walk-ups (on a phone, the Music tab) is a row of colour-coded categories: Warm-up, Pitchers, Crowd favourites and Post-game celebrations. Tap one to see its songs, each a button in the category’s colour.

  • Tap a song to play it (from its start point). Tap it again to fade it out. A new song fades out whatever was playing.
  • Shuffle, the first button, plays a song from that category not played yet this game.
  • Songs already played this game are dimmed and marked Played. New game clears that.

Very short clips (under 5 seconds) play over the music instead of fading it.

An empty category says so. Songs are added in Settings › Team setup › Music (or Admin › Music categories for common songs).

Fade out, Stop and Volume

The bar at the bottom shows what's playing. Fade out fades everything (use it when play starts). Stop cuts everything at once. Volume is the app's own level; the tablet's volume buttons and the speaker matter too.

How does Lock work?

Lock hides Settings (and with it Lineups, Team setup and Admin) so nobody changes the setup by accident. The Game screen, Edit order and Help stay open.

To unlock, press and hold the lock button for a second and a half until it fills up.

Using it on a phone

On a phone the Game screen has two tabs: Walk-ups and Music. It shows the next three batters; tap Show all batters for the whole order, Start from batter 1 and New game. Roster, Songs and Moments show a list first: tap an item to open it, and ‹ Back to return.

Lineups

How do saved lineups work?

A team can keep several lineups (say, one for a doubleheader or a tournament). Each keeps its own order and bench.

  • Settings › Lineups › + New, Duplicate, Rename or Delete (on a phone, these are under ⋯).
  • Use for the game makes it the one the Game screen plays. You can also pick it from the menu at the top of Game.
  • Drag the handle (or use the arrows) to reorder.
  • A new player on the roster joins the end of every lineup.

How do I bench a player for one game?

Tap Bench next to them (on Lineup, or Game › Edit order). They move to the bench and are skipped. Tap their name on the bench (it says Add back) when they're back. A player marked not Active player in Roster sits on every bench.

Setting up a team

How do I add or change a player?Unlocked

Roster › + Add player. Type the First name, Last name and Number; it saves as you type. Untick Active player for someone who's away for a while. Remove from team takes them off the roster and every lineup.

How do I add a player's announcement?

An announcement is a short audio clip, like “Now batting for the Bees… number seven… Jensyn Hiemstra!”. In Roster, tap the player, then 1 · Announcement › Choose a clip and pick the MP3 (M4A and WAV work too). Listen plays it.

Organizers: see How do I make announcement clips?

How do I give a player a walk-up song?

In Roster, tap the player, then 2 · Walk-up song › Choose a song and pick it from the songs on this device (add songs first in Songs). Edit start and stop jumps to that song's clip; Different part for this player changes it for this player only (see below). Test walkup plays the whole thing, announcement and song, the way it sounds in a game.

Under the announcement, or after it?

Song comes in:

  • Under the announcement: the song starts quietly under the announcement, then rises when it ends. Sounds like a big-league stadium.
  • After it: the song starts when the announcement ends.

How quiet, and how fast it rises: Settings › Playback.

What do Ready, No announcement and Not ready mean?

ReadyAnnouncement and song are both on this device.
No announcementThe song plays, with no announcement first.
No songThe announcement plays on its own.
Not readyNothing to play on this device yet. Tap Check for updates on wifi, or add the files in Roster.

How do I add songs?

Songs › + Add songs from this device and pick MP3 files (you can pick several at once). They're saved in the app, so the original files can be moved or deleted afterwards. Type in the search box to find one.

Tap a song to change its Title or Artist, Choose a different file, + Add to a music category, or Delete song.

How do I make a song start at the good part?

  1. In Songs, tap the song. The waveform shows its loud and quiet parts.
  2. Tap Play, or tap the waveform to jump around.
  3. At the catchy part, tap Set start here. Where it should end, tap Set stop here.
  4. Hear the clip plays from start to stop, with the fade.

For exact times, type seconds in Start, Stop and Fade out. Volume (%) turns a loud or quiet song down or up. A walk-up with no stop point plays for the walk-up length in Settings.

How do I add songs to the Music, or change a category?

Team organizers: Settings › Team setup › Music. Tap a category, then + Add songs to pick from your team songs and the common songs. Use the arrows to set the order, or untick Show … on the Game screen to hide a category your team doesn’t use. The common songs in a category are set by the Diamond DJ Admin (shown with a lock).

Diamond DJ Admin: Settings › Admin › Music categories. Rename a category, pick its colour, add common songs for every team, move it earlier or later, add a new category, or delete one (tap twice).

From the Songs screen, + Add to a music category does the same for one song.

Can one player use a different part of a common song?Organizers

Yes. In Settings › Team setup › Roster, tap the player, then Different part for this player. Set Start at and Play for (in seconds), tap Listen to check, then Save. The song itself doesn't change, so every other player and team still hears its own part. Use the song's own part undoes it. Publish to send it to the team.

How do I switch teams or add one?

Tap the team name at the top left. Pick a team to use it, + New team to start one on this device, or Remove to take a team off this device (it stays in Google Drive). Each team has its own roster, lineups, music and game; common songs are shared. The Diamond DJ Admin adds teams for everyone in Admin › Teams (see How do I add a new team?).

How do I change a team's logo and colours?Organizers

Settings › Team setup › Team info › Logo and colours. Choose a picture for the logo (a square PNG with a see-through background looks best; it's made small and kept with the team), then pick the Header colour and the Stripe under the header. The preview shows how the top of the screen will look. The logo shows at the top of the screen and in the team list, and the header takes the team's colour. Buttons keep the Diamond DJ blue and gold so they look the same for every team. Publish to send it to everyone on the team.

What do the Settings do?

Tap the gear. Manage is at the top (Lineups, and Team setup or Admin if you have that role), then four groups that open when you tap them:

UpdatesCheck for updates from Google Drive, and which Google account you’re signed in with.
SoundFade-out length; walk-up length when a song has no stop point; how quiet the song is under the announcement and how fast it comes back up.
This deviceInstall Diamond DJ, storage used, version, Help and what’s new.
AdvancedView as (the Admin trying other roles), and saving or opening a backup file.

Google Drive

What does Check for updates do?

It looks in the team's Google Drive folder for the organizer's latest roster, lineups, music, songs and announcements, and shows what it would download before it does. Only new or changed files are downloaded. Do it at home on wifi before game day.

Will an update undo changes I made?

Lineups you changed on this device are kept: an update never replaces them unless the organizer's version is newer than your change.

If you changed the roster or music on this device, Check for updates warns you first. Tick Keep this device’s roster, team songs and music to keep yours, or leave it unticked to take the organizer's.

Google says “Google hasn't verified this app”.

That's expected while Diamond DJ is in testing. Tap Continue. If Google says you don't have access, ask the organizer to add your Google account (see How do I add a volunteer?).

For organizers

How do I send changes to everyone?Organizers

  1. Make the changes on your device (Roster, Team songs, Music, Lineups).
  2. Settings › Publish. It lists each team you look after (and, for the Diamond DJ Admin, the common songs and music categories) and says which have changes. A gold dot on the gear, and a note at the top of Team setup and Admin, mean something is waiting. Tap Publish next to one. It's there for Google accounts with Editor access to the team's folder.
  3. Check the list of what will be sent (new songs, announcement clips, the team file), then tap Publish.

Volunteers get it the next time they tap Check for updates. Nothing in Drive is deleted, the team file is only replaced after every file uploads, and Drive keeps earlier versions in its version history.

“This Google account can view the team folder but not change it” means the account is a Viewer. Share the folder with it as Editor.

How do I add a volunteer or organizer?Admin

  1. Settings › Admin › People, and sign in with your Google account if asked.
  2. Under Add someone, type their email, pick Volunteer, Team organizer · (team) or Diamond DJ Admin, and tap Add. Google emails them a link with how to get started.
  3. While the app is in testing, add them in Google Cloud too: console.cloud.google.com › Diamond DJ project › Google Auth Platform › Audience › Test users › Add users. (Up to 100.)

On the same screen you can make a volunteer a team organizer or an Admin, remove someone, and fix the warnings it shows: someone who can't get the common songs (they're on a team folder but not the Diamond DJ folder: tap Fix), or a folder anyone with the link can open (tap Make it private). Team organizers can't change sharing; they ask the Admin.

Who can do what?

Roles come from how the Google Drive folders are shared:

  • Diamond DJ Admin: Editor on the Diamond DJ folder. Common songs, music categories, every team, and who has access (Settings › Admin).
  • Team organizer: Editor on their team's folder (and Viewer on the Diamond DJ folder). Their team's roster, walk-ups, team songs, music, logo and colours (Settings › Team setup).
  • Volunteer: Viewer on the Diamond DJ folder. Gets the common songs and any team with Check for updates, and runs the music on game day.

Everyone can change lineups on their own device. Roles are read at each Check for updates.

How do I add a new team?Admin

  1. Settings › Admin › Teams › + Add a team.
  2. Name and look: the team name, a short name for the top of the screen, and (if you have them) a logo and colours.
  3. How it starts: empty, or with the music copied from another team (its team songs and the songs it added to music categories; not its players).
  4. Who has access: the team's organizers and volunteers (you can add people later too), then Make the team.
  5. Diamond DJ goes straight to publishing the team: tap Publish now. That makes the team's folder in Google Drive and then shares it with the people you added.

Admin › Teams also shows every team on this device: how many players and team songs, whether it has changes to publish, its organizers, and buttons to open its setup, logo and colours, or Google Drive folder.

What's in the Google Drive folder?Organizers

Diamond DJ/
├── library.diamond.json      the common songs and music categories
├── Common Songs/             the common songs, for every team
└── U13 Bees/                 one folder per team
    ├── U13-Bees.diamond.json roster, walk-ups, team songs, music, logo and colours
    ├── Team Songs/           the team's own songs
    └── Announcements/        one clip per player

Let the app manage these files: add songs and clips in the app, then Publish. A new team's folder is made by its first Publish.

How do I make announcement clips?Organizers

Any MP3 works: record one on a phone, or make one with a text-to-speech service such as ElevenLabs (a stadium announcer voice, with a little echo). Keep the same wording for everyone, for example “Now batting for the Bees… number seven… Jensyn Hiemstra!”, and keep it under about 8 seconds.

Name each file after the player (for example #7 - Jensyn Hiemstra.mp3), add it in Roster › Choose a clip, check it with Test walkup, then Publish.

How do I back up a team?Organizers

Settings › Advanced › Save a backup saves the roster, lineups, song names and cut points in a small file. The music isn't in it: Google Drive keeps that. Open a backup brings a team back.

Problems

Nothing plays.

  • Check the tablet's volume, the Volume slider at the bottom of the app, and the speaker.
  • Bluetooth: connect the speaker before opening the app. If it was connected after, close the app and open it again.
  • A batter marked Not ready, or a song marked File not on this device, isn't downloaded yet: Settings › Check for updates on wifi.

The sound is a little late on the speaker.

Bluetooth always adds a short delay. Tap a song as soon as it’s needed. A cable to the sound system has no delay.

The screen turns off during the game.

Diamond DJ keeps the screen on while it's open, where the device allows it. If it still turns off: Android: Settings › Display › Screen timeout › the longest time. iPad / iPhone: Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock › Never.

The device is running out of space.

Settings › This device (at the bottom) shows how much the app uses. Remove teams you don't run (team button › Remove) and songs nobody uses (Songs › the song › Delete song).

It says “A new version of Diamond DJ is ready”.

A new version downloaded in the background. Tap See what's new to read what changed, then Update now when nothing is playing. Your teams, lineups and songs stay. Later keeps the current version until the app is next opened.

The message waits until nothing is playing. Small fixes install by themselves with no message; the screen may blink for a second.

Which version do I have, and what changed?

Tap the ? at the top. The version is the first line of Help. What's new lists the latest updates, and App history lists every one. When asking the organizer for help, send them that version line and what happened.
